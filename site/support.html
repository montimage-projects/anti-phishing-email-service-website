Support & feedback

A wrong verdict, a problem, an idea: tell us.

Write to us by email, or open an issue on GitHub. Either way, a person at Montimage reads it.

Before you write

Do not send support requests to antiphishing@montimage.eu. That mailbox is read by the service, not by a person: whatever arrives there is analysed as a suspicious email and answered with a verdict.

Write to developer@montimage.eu instead.

Private

Email

Public

GitHub issues are public. Never paste the suspicious email, its headers, email addresses, links or attachments into an issue. Give the Reference code printed at the foot of your report instead. If we need the email itself, send it to developer@montimage.eu with the issue number.
