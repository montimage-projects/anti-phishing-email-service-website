Privacy policy

What happens to your data, in plain words.

This page covers this website, the emails you forward to the free trial, self-hosted deployments, and the messages you send us for support.

Who is responsible

Montimage, 39 rue Bobillot, 75013 Paris, France, is responsible for the data described on this page. For any privacy question or request, write to developer@montimage.eu.

This website

  • The site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, and has no forms.
  • Fonts and images are served from the site itself, so your browser contacts no other service when you read it.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the technical data every web request carries, such as your IP address, and handles it under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Emails you forward to the free trial

When you forward an email to antiphishing@montimage.eu, it is analysed by the instance of the service that Montimage runs, and the verdict is sent back to the address you forwarded from.

What we receive and keep

  • The forwarded email: its text, headers, sender and recipients, the links it contains and its attachments.
  • Your email address and the time you forwarded it, so we can send the reply.
  • The analysis results and the report we send back.

Why

We use this data to analyse the email and answer you, which is the service you ask for when you forward it. We also use it to keep the service secure and working, and to improve how it detects phishing. We do not use your address for marketing, and we do not sell or rent your data.

Who else receives it

Some checks rely on outside services. Each one receives only what it needs to answer:

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
TypeSafe and a hosted AI model providerThe text of the email, including its subject and senderAI review for deception and impersonation
VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB and AlienVault OTXURLs, domain names and IP addresses found in the emailChecking them against known threats

These providers process the data under their own terms, and some of them are based outside the European Union.

How long we keep it

Forwarded emails and their analysis results are kept for 90 days, then deleted automatically. To have an email deleted sooner, write to developer@montimage.eu and give the Reference code printed at the foot of your report.

Forward only what you are allowed to share. Forwarding an email shares it, and the personal data it contains, with Montimage and the providers above. For confidential mail, run the service on your own infrastructure instead.

Self-hosted deployments

When your organisation runs the service on its own infrastructure, your organisation controls the data it processes. The service sends nothing to Montimage. It contacts an AI provider or a threat-intelligence feed only if your organisation enables that integration.

Support and feedback

  • Email. When you write to developer@montimage.eu, we use your message and address to answer you and keep the exchange for as long as it is needed to follow up.
  • GitHub issues. Issues on our GitHub repository are public, and GitHub handles your account data under its own privacy statement. Never post a suspicious email, its headers or anyone’s personal data in an issue. See Support & feedback for how to share a sample privately.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive a copy of it. Write to developer@montimage.eu. We answer within one month.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the French data-protection authority, the CNIL.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date below.
